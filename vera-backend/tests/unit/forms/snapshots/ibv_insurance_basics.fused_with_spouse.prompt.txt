Verify the member's plan identity and network status. Confirm the spouse's identity only when the policy is a family plan, and record answers exactly as the representative states them.

### Insurance Information
1. Is the doctor inside the insurance network?
   - Answers: Yes | No
2. Is the facility inside the insurance network?
   - Answers: Yes | No
3. Does this plan cover out-of-network benefits?
   - Answers: Yes | No
   - Ask only if "Doctor Inside Network" is "No" and "Facility Inside Network" is "No".
4. What type of plan is this — PPO, HMO, EPO, or something else?
   - Also accepted: PPO, HMO, EPO, POS
5. For coordination of benefits, is this plan primary, secondary, or tertiary?
   - Answers: Primary | Secondary | Tertiary
6. ask — Can I get the member ID for this policy?
7. What is the group name?
8. What is the group number?
9. What is the policy state or contract state?
   - Hint: If the representative doesn't understand the question, clarify: In which state was this policy written?
Ask together on the first pass: "What type of plan is this — PPO, HMO, EPO, or something else? And for coordination of benefits, is this plan primary, secondary, or tertiary?" (covers: Health Plan Type, Coordination of Benefits).
Ask together on the first pass: "What is the group name and group number?" (covers: Group Name, Group Number).

### Benefit Coverage
10. Does the benefit year run on a Calendar Year or a Plan Year?
   - Answers: Calendar Year | Plan Year
11. What is the effective date for this coverage?
   - Expected date format: M/D/YYYY
   - When "Benefit Year Type" is "Calendar Year": record "01/01/2026" without asking.
12. What are the start and end dates of the plan year?
   - When "Benefit Year Type" is "Calendar Year": record "01/01/2026 - 12/31/2026" without asking.
13. Is this an individual or a family policy? Please answer Individual or Family.
   - Answers: Individual | Family
   - Immediately after this answer:
     * If "Coverage Type" is "Family": confirm — Can we also check the spouse on the plan? I have the spouse listed as Jane Doe — can you confirm that is correct?
     * If "Coverage Type" is "Family": confirm — And the spouse's date of birth I have is April 12, 1991 — is that right?
14. Does this plan require a PCP referral?
   - Answers: Yes | No
   - Ask only if "Health Plan Type" is "HMO".
15. Does this plan cover telehealth services?
   - Answers: Yes | No | Limited
   - Hint: If the representative asks whether we use our own telehealth platform, answer exactly "Yes", then re-ask this question.
16. Is this plan self insured or fully funded?
   - Answers: Self Insured | Fully Funded
17. Is the employer group supporting this plan a small group or a large group?
   - Answers: Small Group | Large Group
18. Is there an infertility plan mandate on this policy?
   - Answers: Yes | No
   - Hint: Pronounce as: in-fer-TIL-ih-tee plan MAN-date.

TERMINATION RULE — no_out_of_network_coverage:
If "Doctor Inside Network" is "No" and "Facility Inside Network" is "No" and "Out-of-Network Coverage" is "No": Both the doctor and facility are out of network and the plan has no out-of-network coverage. Skip all remaining tasks, collect the representative name and call reference number, then end the call. Stop the remaining questions and move to "Wrap Up".

CONSISTENCY CHECK — small_group_self_insured_conflict:
If "Employer Support Size" is "Small Group" and "Plan Fund Type" is "Self Insured": Small group plans are typically fully insured, not self-funded. Push back once, saying: "I want to make sure I have this right — I have the plan as self insured and the employer as a small group, but small group plans are typically fully insured. Could you double-check whether the plan is self insured or fully funded, and whether the employer group is small or large?" Then re-confirm: Plan Fund Type, Employer Support Size.
